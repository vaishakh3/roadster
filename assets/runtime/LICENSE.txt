Derivation / encoding
=====================
Only resolution, normal-vector resampling and compression were changed. No
paint-over, recoloring, exposure adjustment, relighting or invented texture
content was applied. Original source downloads and their checksums remain in
assets/realism/ and assets/realism/LICENSE.txt.

The image encoder was the app's installed Python runtime, Pillow 12.3.0 with
WebP support; NumPy 2.3.5 was used for vector resampling. The runtime is located
at $HOME/.cache/codex-runtimes/codex-primary-runtime/dependencies/python/bin/python3.
Equivalent Pillow encoding operations, with method=6 and exact=True throughout:

- asphalt_color.webp: original RGB, 1024 x 1024, WebP quality=82.
- asphalt_normal.webp: original RGB data converted to vectors with
  v = RGB / 127.5 - 1; average each 2 x 2 block in this linear vector space;
  normalize each averaged vector; round(clamp((v + 1) * 127.5, 0, 255)) back to
  uint8 RGB; save 512 x 512 with lossless=True and quality=100. No sRGB transfer
  function is applied. The post-quantization vectors have lengths 0.9956–1.0048;
  the 95th-percentile direction quantization error is 0.283 degrees.
- asphalt_roughness.webp: convert to L, resize 512 x 512 with Pillow LANCZOS,
  WebP quality=90. The encoded image decodes to neutral RGB grayscale.
- sandstone_color.webp: original RGB resized 512 x 512 with Pillow LANCZOS,
  WebP quality=94.

The HDR was decoded with the repository's Three.js 0.180.0 HDRLoader using
setDataType(FloatType). Every 2 x 2 group of radiance samples was averaged in
linear floating-point RGB to form 512 x 256 pixels. No tone mapping, gamma or
exposure adjustment was performed. Samples were re-encoded as Radiance RGBE
with the standard FORMAT=32-bit_rle_rgbe header and per-scanline channel RLE.
The encoder uses a shared exponent e = floor(log2(max(R,G,B))) + 1 and rounded
RGB * 255 / 2^e bytes, matching HDRLoader's inverse channel scale. The file was
re-parsed with the shipped HDRLoader and checked for the expected dimensions
and finite radiance. Summed decoded RGB energy differs from the linear average
by +0.0507% due to RGBE quantization. Averaging naturally lowers very small
highlight peaks while preserving their integrated radiance.

Sampling and integration
========================
- Use image/webp for embedded WebP data URLs and image/vnd.radiance (or the
  existing binary HDR data URL MIME) for the HDR. The shipped HDRLoader can read
  this HDR without any implementation changes.
- asphalt_color and sandstone_color are color maps: use THREE.SRGBColorSpace.
- asphalt_normal and asphalt_roughness are data maps: use THREE.NoColorSpace.
  The normal map remains OpenGL (+Y); do not invert its green channel.
- Keep corresponding map repeat/rotation/offset/flipY settings identical.
  Use RepeatWrapping, mipmapped minification and anisotropy 4–8 where available.
- Keep the HDR loader's linear color/type configuration, and use PMREM for the
  shared scene environment. Retain one shared environment across venue changes.
- These encodings trade invisible/distant microdetail for transfer and decode
  cost. Normal/rock/roughness details are softer under close inspection than the
  1K source. HDR highlights and small reflections are softer than the 1K source.
  They are intended for the racing camera and the existing roughness values;
  retain original source files for any future extreme close-up presentation.

Payload: 689132 bytes before HTML/base64/gzip overhead (the originals total
2753561 bytes). Exact RGBA8 full-mip allocation estimate: 5330244 bytes
(5.08 MiB). These WebPs are transfer compressed, not GPU compressed. Decoder
backing images add approximately 3997696 bytes of RGBA8 storage before any
browser retention/copy overhead. Three textures are loaded once and reused.

Only size/format/vector-resampling changed in the derivative image files.
No image paint-over or invented texture content was applied. Slate coloration,
subtle moss on upward-facing surfaces and macro variation are material shader
decisions. The original 2 m scan scale is preserved by world triplanar sampling.

The dedicated cliff material uses 3 base-color, 3 normal, 3 packed ARM and
2 shared detail samples per visible fragment; it adds no draws or geometry.
The renderer applies physical direct/environment lighting and receiveShadow;
the scanned AO and optional coastalAO vertex attribute affect indirect light.
The normal strength is deliberately low (.12) to avoid sparkling microrelief.
All three projections use the same 29-degree rotation and continuous spatial
warp across their color/normal/ARM maps to break conspicuous repeated rows.
It introduces no extra lights, shadow maps, render targets or postprocessing.

The optional coastal road profile reuses the existing asphalt maps. Its profile
is zero outside the authored region and fades smoothly over 80 m at each end.
The in-region scan scale is 2 m per tile. Irregular moisture changes albedo and
roughness (minimum .56), with understated wheel-track wear. It has no animated
light bands or rectangular reflection decal meshes. The opted-in road shader
adds 2 shared detail samples, 1 color and 1 normal sample inside the region.
